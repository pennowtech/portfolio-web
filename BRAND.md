# ArchitectAtWork Brand Notes

## Brand

**ArchitectAtWork**

ArchitectAtWork presents the practical work, engineering perspective, and technical writing of Sukhdeep Singh. The
name intentionally connects architectural thinking with hands-on implementation.

## Primary slogan

**Architecture grounded in engineering.**

This is the default line for the website header, footer, social profiles, and concise brand introductions.

## Supporting slogans

- Architecture in practice, not just on paper.
- Designing systems. Delivering software.
- Where architectural thinking meets hands-on engineering.
- From system boundaries to working software.
- Architecture that earns its place in production.
- Built to work. Designed to last.
- Engineering depth behind every design.
- Complex systems, clearly designed.
- From design intent to dependable delivery.
- Systems, software, and decisions—made visible.

## Visual direction

- Graphite provides the structural foundation.
- Forest green identifies actions, active states, and the `AtWork` portion of the name.
- Orange remains a restrained supporting accent where it already carries meaning.
- Blue is not part of the ArchitectAtWork interface palette.
- The `A/W` monogram uses structural strokes rather than a decorative icon container.
