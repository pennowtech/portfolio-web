# SinghBuildsTech

SinghBuildsTech is mainly portfolio website.

The portfolio lists down together my practical architecture experience, selected engineering projects, technical skills, and
long-form writing about building software that works beyond the diagram. Plan is to also to pen down my hobbies and travels here.

## Explore the portfolio

- **About:** professional background and working approach
- **Skills:** architecture, modelling, engineering, platform, and middleware capabilities
- **Projects:** selected public and private work
- **Articles:** practical technical writing sourced from Notion
- **Contact:** a protected form for project and professional conversations

## Privacy and accessibility

The site provides a dedicated Privacy Policy and Imprint. Optional Google Analytics remains disabled until affirmative
consent is provided. Google reCAPTCHA is limited to pages containing the contact form, and website fonts are served
locally rather than fetched from Google Fonts.

The interface supports light and dark themes, keyboard navigation, responsive phone and tablet layouts, and readable
content widths across screen sizes.
